Good questions

Before you ask.

Still curious? Send a message and Christopher will reply personally.

How much does a custom piece cost?

It depends on size, materials and detail. Share a budget range in the form and you will get an estimate before any work begins.

How long does a custom order take?

Resin needs time to cure properly, so timing varies by piece. You will get a clear timeline in your estimate. Mention any deadline in your request.

Can I choose the colors and the letter?

Yes. Colors, initials, names and finishes are all part of the conversation. Send inspiration photos if you have them.

Do the pieces come with lights?

Light-up pieces can include built-in lighting. Ask about options when you request your quote.

Do you ship, or is it local pickup?

Ask in your request and we will confirm delivery or pickup options for your location.
